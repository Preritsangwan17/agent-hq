About the Role


We’re looking for an AI / GenAI who is genuinely curious about how modern AI systems work and enjoys experimenting with LLMs, AI tools, agents, and automation workflows.

You’ll work closely with the GenAI course and content team, helping turn AI concepts and industry practices into practical, learner-friendly content and experiences.


This is a hands-on role for someone who likes building, experimenting, researching, and figuring things out, rather than someone who only knows AI concepts theoretically.


Key Responsibilities


  • Explore and work with LLMs, AI models, AI tools, and emerging GenAI technologies
  • Experiment with AI agents, tool calling, workflows, orchestration, and multi-agent systems
  • Research and understand emerging GenAI tools, frameworks, platforms, and industry use cases
  • Build and experiment with AI-powered workflows, prototypes, and automations
  • Explore concepts such as RAG, APIs, function/tool calling, agents, memory, evaluation, and LLM application development
  • Use AI tools to research, create, review, and improve technical learning content
  • Assist in creating session documents, assignments, coding exercises, quizzes, assessments, and learner resources
  • Review AI/technical content for accuracy, clarity, practical relevance, and consistency
  • Convert technical concepts and experiments into simple, structured explanations and learning material


AI / Technical Exposure


You should be comfortable with or actively exploring areas such as:

  • Generative AI: LLMs, prompting, context windows, embeddings, RAG, multimodal AI
  • AI Agents: Agents, tool/function calling, agent workflows, memory, planning, multi-agent systems
  • AI Development: Python, APIs, SDKs, LLM application development, basic backend concepts
  • AI Tools & Platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, or similar AI development tools

Knowledge of frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen, or similar technologies is a plus.


Who Should Apply?


We’re looking for someone who:

  • Has a strong interest in Generative AI and emerging AI technologies
  • Has a foundational understanding of LLMs, AI agents, tools, and orchestration
  • Has experimented with AI tools, APIs, agents, or AI-powered applications
  • Is comfortable learning new AI tools and technologies quickly
  • Has good Python programming skills
  • Understands how to work with APIs and is interested in building AI-powered applications
  • Enjoys hands-on experimentation and prototyping
  • Can research unfamiliar AI concepts and understand how they work
  • Has strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Is comfortable working in an environment where tools, technologies, and requirements evolve rapidly


Good to Have


  • Personal AI/GenAI projects or prototypes
  • GitHub projects involving LLMs, agents, RAG, APIs, or AI automation
  • Experience with agentic frameworks or orchestration tools
  • Experience building AI-powered applications
  • Understanding of LLM evaluation, observability, or production AI workflows


Why Join Us?


  • Work hands-on with modern GenAI technologies and AI development workflows
  • Get exposure to LLMs, agents, orchestration, RAG, APIs, and AI tools
  • Work on real-world AI curriculum, projects, experiments, and learning experiences
  • Experiment with emerging AI tools and frameworks
  • Learn how AI concepts are translated into practical, industry-relevant learning experiences
  • Work closely with AI instructors, SMEs, and content developers
  • Opportunity to contribute to broader AI, technical content, and product initiatives
  • Opportunity for extension based on performance and business requirements
