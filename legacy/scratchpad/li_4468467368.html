About the Role

We are looking for a passionate and curious Product Engineer Intern to join our team building next-generation AI agentic systems. The ideal candidate will help design and ship intelligent, autonomous product features while gaining hands-on experience with LLMs, agent frameworks, and modern cloud infrastructure.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and iterate on AI agent workflows and product features powered by LLMs.
  • Develop high-quality, maintainable full-stack applications that integrate agentic capabilities into the product.
  • Prototype and test agent behaviors — tool use, memory, multi-step reasoning, and orchestration.
  • Collaborate with product, design, and engineering teams to define, design, and ship new AI-driven features.
  • Analyze agent performance, identify failure modes, and improve reliability, latency, and cost efficiency.
  • Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve issues across the application and agent pipeline.
  • Contribute to evaluation frameworks and testing strategies for agentic systems.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree (or pursuing) in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design principles.
  • Hands-on experience in one or more of the following:
  • React.js, Node.js, Python
  • LLM APIs / agent frameworks (e.g., LangChain, LlamaIndex, OpenAI/Anthropic SDKs, or similar)
  • AWS Lambda and serverless architectures
  • Genuine interest in AI agents, product thinking, and building user-facing intelligent systems.
  • Prior exposure to prompt engineering, RAG, or vector databases is a plus.
  • AWS Certification is a plus.

Stipend and Work Hours

  • ₹25,000 per month
  • Duration: 6 months
  • 20 hours per week (part-time)
  • Exceptional interns may be offered a full-time Product Engineer / SDE role upon successful completion of the internship, with a competitive annual salary package.
