Machine Learning Intern

Location: Bangalore

Employment Type: Internship

Full-Time Opportunity: Based on performance


About the Role

We are looking for a highly motivated and analytical AI/ML & Strategy Exploration Intern to join our team. The role involves working with data, supporting Machine Learning activities, exploring data-driven strategies, and solving analytical problems.

The ideal candidate should have strong problem-solving skills, fundamental knowledge of Machine Learning, and a good foundation in Mathematics and Statistics.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare, clean, organize, and analyze datasets for AI/ML and strategy-related projects.
  • Perform data preprocessing and basic statistical analysis.
  • Assist with manual evaluation and validation of ML model outputs.
  • Explore datasets to identify patterns, trends, relationships, and potential signals.
  • Support the exploration and evaluation of data-driven strategies.
  • Apply fundamental Mathematics, Statistics, and Machine Learning concepts to practical problems.
  • Assist in testing and validating models, strategies, and analytical results.
  • Identify data-quality issues, inconsistencies, and meaningful insights.
  • Document findings, observations, and analysis clearly.
  • Work closely with the AI/ML and research teams on ongoing projects.
  • Take ownership of assigned problems and approach them with structured and logical thinking.

Requirements

  • Education: B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
  • Graduation Year: 2026 pass-out candidates only.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Good understanding of fundamental Machine Learning concepts.
  • Strong foundation in Mathematics and Statistics.
  • Good logical and quantitative reasoning ability.
  • Basic knowledge of Python.
  • Familiarity with NumPy, Pandas, or similar data-processing tools is preferred.
  • Ability to analyze data and interpret results.
  • Curiosity and willingness to learn and work on challenging problems.

Good to Have

  • Exposure to Machine Learning, Data Science, Quantitative Research, or Financial Markets.
  • Experience with academic, personal, or internship projects involving ML, statistics, mathematics, or data analysis.

Eligibility

  • B.Tech/B.E. – 2026 batch graduates.
  • Candidates from Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering, or related disciplines.
  • Candidates with strong mathematical, analytical, and problem-solving skills are encouraged to

apply.
